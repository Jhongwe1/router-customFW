### Settled before any script

1. **The CPU port's histogram does count 64-byte frames.** `LOG.md`
   第一百一十段's E1 row says the two 64-byte ARP frames were not counted; 量
   on the raw lines, the CPU port's `64:` bucket reads 0, 2, 25 at `D1-K0`,
   `D1-K1`, `D1-K2`. The bench-night parser's pattern for `64:` matched inside
   `< 64:`, so its "64" column was the `< 64` bucket. The claim enters no
   `SPEC.md` row; its correction lands with block 45's record.
2. **Port 3's output is `CRCAlignErr` − `JabberErr` − `Drop` − `FragErr`**,
   exactly, in E2 (172) and E4 (1,136) (量). Block 45's card left `FragErr`
   out of that identity, and it is off by one in both.
3. **The `tx` verb shares `nic_xmit`'s descriptor lengths and none of its
   path** (讀 `nic_do_tx`): `m_len` = frame, `ph_len` = frame + 4, `m_extsize`
   = 2,046, the same buffer offset; no `nic_lock`, and the same TX index as
   `nic_xmit`. So the stack must be silent while `tx` runs, and a difference
   between the two can only be the stack's path — never the descriptor
   lengths, which need an image (`R6b-2`).
4. **rlxfw's descriptor lengths differ from both vendor paths** (讀): the
   vendor's `_swNic_send` sets `m_len` = `m_extsize` = `ph_len` = frame + 4 (64
   for a runt); rlxfw sets `m_len` 4 short of `ph_len` and `m_extsize`
   constant. `docs/nic-vendor-diff.md`, the field-by-field comparison `R6`'s
   `D3` refutation asked for, had no row for either field until `R6b-0` added
   § 1's rows 11–12. The loader sets all three equal as well (讀, its send
   routine at `0x80403CF0`, from the local dump by the owner's leave; that
   file's § 16, `NET-122`), so rlxfw is the one writer of the four that differs.
5. **A frame refused while the ring is full is dropped, not requeued** (讀,
   `NET-57`: `tx_queue_len 0` makes `NETDEV_TX_BUSY` a drop). The driver's own
   comment that the stack requeues it is wrong on this image; `R6b-2`'s driver
   corrects it.
6. **Cache coherency is not a candidate** (讀 `rtl819x-nic.c`): every TX byte,
   descriptor and buffer is in KSEG1, uncached, so the vendor's per-frame
   writeback has nothing to be missing from rlxfw (`CPU-45`).
7. **Removing the vendor tree removes three things this gate reads** (讀):
   `/proc/rtl865x/asicCounter`, the instrument of every TX bracket;
   `/proc/rtl865x/port_status`, `MT-PORT`'s only source (`docs/mfgtest.md`
   § 2); and `eth4`, the control driver and `NET-25`'s subject. So `R6b-8`'s
   removal (8g) comes after `R6b-1`…`R6b-4` and D1–D4, after `R6b-6` gives `MT-PORT` another
   source, and after `NET-25`'s ten cold boots, which ride 8c's seating (the owner, 2026-09-26).
8. **The owner's rulings, 2026-09-25:** no segment cap; the loader's TX
   routine may be disassembled from the local dump, nothing of it entering the
   repository but the rule and the addresses, with a read at the loader
   prompt only if the desk cannot settle it; `R6b-1`'s card is declared for
   2026-09-25, one press.
