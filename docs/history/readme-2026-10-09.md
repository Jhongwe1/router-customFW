# `README.md`'s lead and `Status` row, as they stood through `v0.6`

Moved verbatim out of `README.md` on 2026-10-09, when `v1.0` was released and the lead's third
bullet and the `Status` row were rewritten to the current state. Both places are as they stood at
`1b13303d`: the lead as `v0.6` shipped it, and the `Status` row as `v0.6` shipped it and then

## The lead (`README.md` lines 7–17 through `v0.6`)

- 🟢 **rlxfw's own userspace runs on this router.** Six programs of rlxfw's own — `init` as PID 1, a
  config store, a privileged broker, a web server, a DNS forwarder, a lease handler — and a busybox
  rebuilt to a chosen applet set ran on the die from a RAM boot and answered: `httpd` as uid 100 in a
  chroot, and `dnsfwd` as uid 101, printing `setuid(0) refused (Operation not permitted)` because it
  checks its own privilege drop by trying to undo it ([`docs/GATE-RESULTS.md`](docs/GATE-RESULTS.md) entry 17).
- 🟢 **A signed update is verified on the silicon before it runs.** `rlxboot` booted a correct container,
  refused one whose payload had a single bit flipped — `SIG ok`, then `DIGEST bad` — and refused a
  version below the anti-rollback counter (entry 16). All of it was staged in RAM: writing an update
  to flash, and cutting the power during the write, are `R8b`'s, closed 2026-10-08 (entry 20).
- 🟢 **One unit, no spare, and no write command sent to its flash.** `R7` and `R8a` ran from RAM after
  a TFTP upload, and their console captures are committed under [`bench/2026-09-30/`](bench/2026-09-30).
