### The DoD, split into what can be refuted

* **D1** six drivers in-tree, each accepted, **10 boots without an oops** each.
* **D2** each has a compile-tested DT binding, **marked *not probed on hardware***.
* **D3** 🔄 **restated 2026-09-02 at `R5-0`, into two layers scored
  separately**, because the original surface could not measure what it was
  chosen to measure. `docs/driver-diff.md` exists, written blind first, and
  each row is scored on the layer it belongs to:
  **L1 fact** — register addresses, reset values, documented bit positions.
  Three implementations describing *one piece of silicon*; agreement is
  expected and says nothing about independence, so this layer is a
  **cross-check**, not a diff.
  **L2 decision** — divisor semantics (`CLK-06`, 推), wrap handling for a
  modulus that is not a power of two, interrupt number, which bits are
  write-1-to-clear, initialisation order, `clocksource` `mult`/`shift`,
  rating and coexistence. **This is the layer the ledger protects**, and every
  L2 row carries a verdict or an explicit 未定 with the experiment that would
  settle it.
  🔴 **The objection to this change is recorded with it**: `docs/GATE-RESULTS.md`'s
  operating clause warns that changing a rule because it failed to produce the
  answer you had already reached is how such a rule stops being an instrument.
  The distinction claimed — no conclusion had been reached; what was found is
  that **L1 agreement is guaranteed by the parts being the same part**, which
  is instrument validity — and **the test that settles it: the new `D3` is
  EASIER to fail.** *Register by register* passes almost by construction;
  the two-layer form lets `R5-0`'s ledger **void** rows outright (§ 4.5).
  A change that makes a DoD more refutable is not a target being moved.
* **D4** the timer is the system time base and `/proc/timer_list` agrees with
  `CLK-02` to ± 50 ppm.

🔴 **`D1`'s "10 boots without an oops" is the row most likely to be fudged, and
it is written here with the fudge named**: ten boots of *the same image* proves
less than ten boots across ten builds, and a driver that is never exercised
cannot oops. **Each driver's ten boots must include the thing the driver does** —
the timer keeping time, the GPIO toggling a pin, MTD reading 4 MiB — or the
number is a boot count and not an acceptance.

### Refutation condition, written now

> **否證 `D4`** — the two clocksources diverging in `R5-2` refutes the divisor
> chain, not the driver. That is a finding about `CLK-06` and it goes back there
> rather than being patched around in the driver.

> **否證 `D3`** — if `R5-0`'s ledger shows the register maps were already read,
> `driver-diff.md` **is not written**, and the gate says so. A diff between two
> implementations, one of which saw the other, is a diff of one implementation.
> 🔄 **2026-09-02, sharpened by the ledger rather than softened by it, and it
> now fires per driver instead of per document**: `docs/blind-write-ledger.md`
> § 4.5 records the vendor's MTD map read on the **decision** layer, so
> **`R5-5`'s L2 rows are void unless the derivation check clears them**, and
> the document says so in place rather than omitting the section. The four
> drivers with zero cited implementations — `gpio`, `wdt`, `led`, `keys` — are
> unaffected, and that split is the ledger's whole output.

> **否證 `D1`** — a driver that boots ten times and is never exercised is not
> accepted. Each acceptance names the observable the driver itself produces.

### Stop-loss, written now

* **More than 34 segments** (24 猜 + the 1.4× this board's two comparable gates
  ran at) and the remaining drivers move to `R10a` with the count recorded.
* 🔴 **If `R5-1`'s coexistence experiment cannot be built** — if 2.6.30's
  clocksource core will not read two sources — the timer step is re-scoped to a
  measurement against `CLK-02` alone and the loss of the single-variable
  experiment is recorded, **not hidden by declaring the reading good enough**.
* 🔴 **`--keep` still may not be turned on to hit a number.** `P4a`'s `L2-6`
  costs 2 bytes for a same-width link and **56** across a digit boundary; if the
  incremental build becomes the answer here, it arrives with that re-measured
  and `notes/reproducible-build.md` updated in the same commit, or it does not
  arrive.
* **If the first driver takes more than 6 segments**, the estimate is
  renegotiated in the open before the second one starts — `24 ÷ 6 = 4` per
  driver is the implied budget and nothing has tested it.
