# `README.md`'s lead, `Status` row and `Not measured` row, as they stood through `v0.5`

Moved verbatim out of `README.md` on 2026-10-04, when `v0.6` was released and these three places
were rewritten to the current state. The lead and the `Not measured` row are as `v0.5` shipped
them; the `Status` row is as it stood at `90f72d99`, edited in place after `v0.5` to say that
`main` carried `R7` and `R8` unreleased. `tools/docmove.py` proves the move.

## The lead (`README.md` lines 7–17 through `v0.5`)

- 🟢 **rlxfw's own kernel drivers carry this router's Ethernet, with none of the vendor's
  Ethernet code in the image.** After a cold power-on and again after a warm reboot, `rlx0`
  answered ping both ways and the host resolved rlxfw's own locally administered address, with
  no command typed after boot ([`docs/GATE-RESULTS.md`](docs/GATE-RESULTS.md) entry 14, `NET-167`).
- 🟢 **The transmit fault that lost frames at eleven lengths is found and fixed as the default.**
  Single-variable A/B put it in the length fields the driver wrote into its DMA descriptors; with
  the vendor's convention those lengths answered 220 of 220 on two boots on which the old one
  answered 145 of 1,327 and 144 of 1,189. Why the engine damages the old lengths is still open.
- 🟢 **One unit, no spare: the evidence is in this repository.** Every number above is read from a
  committed capture of that board — arm I's re-derived by a reader that shares no code with the run's
  own verdict — and a tool refuses any command that can write its flash unless the owner's dated yes is on the card.
