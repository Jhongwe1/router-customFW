# ADR-008: Drivers written blind first, then diffed against the two public RTL8196E trees

## Context
`R5` wrote rlxfw's first six drivers for this SoC, and two public ports of it exist, `shibajee/linux-rtl8196e` and `ggbruno/openwrt`. Register addresses, reset values and bit positions are facts, and three implementations of one piece of silicon are expected to agree on them. Divisor semantics, wrap handling, which bits are write-1-to-clear, initialisation order, rating and coexistence are choices, "the only layer on which a diff means anything" (`docs/driver-diff.md` § 0).
## Decision
Before the first line of driver source existed, `docs/blind-write-ledger.md` froze what the repository had read of every external implementation, "because afterwards *'I had not read it'* is unverifiable" (2026-09-02, `R5-0`); `tools/ledgerscan.py check` fails while any external path the repository cites has no row in it. The public trees were read only after all six drivers had run on the silicon, and the first operation on each was a derivation check against the vendor's `arch/rlx`, pre-registered in `docs/driver-diff.md` § 1 and committed 85 seconds before the first byte of either tree reached disk. A driver whose decision needed vendor code leaves the ledger's blind section, and its diff is never called blind (`CLAUDE.md`).
## Alternatives considered
- **A declaration in prose of what had been read**: a hand-typed grep returned 0 files, and the sentence about to be written from it was false: "Zero citations is not zero reading, and a zero on one spelling says nothing about another." The ledger became a tool with a positive control (`docs/blind-write-ledger.md` § 1).
- **Taking the public ports as independent unchecked**: if they derived from the vendor's `arch/rlx`, reading the vendor would have been reading their ancestor, and the independence would be gone without a third-party file opened (`docs/blind-write-ledger.md` § 6).
- **Staying blind of the vendor for every driver**: given up for the watchdog, on purpose, because its central decision was `CONFIG_RTL_WTDOG=n` "and nobody can decide to remove code they have not read" (`docs/blind-write-ledger.md` § 4.1).
## Consequences
- 量 the derivation check: a positive control at 175 identifiers and three negative controls at 0; ten of eleven candidate domains independent, and the one derived file, `ggbruno`'s `prom.c`, is outside the six drivers, so no row of the diff is void (`docs/GATE-RESULTS.md` entry 8).
- The blind section, § 4.1, now holds `R5-7` (LEDs) and `R5-8` (keys); the timer's own section records its contamination as nil where it matters (§ 4.3). GPIO, the watchdog and SPI + MTD read vendor code, and the watchdog's decision-layer rows are informed contrast, "not *evidence of independent convergence*, which is what a blind diff buys" (`docs/blind-write-ledger.md` § 4.5, § 4.9, § 4.10.1).
- The ledger records what the repository wrote down, not what was read, so it is a lower bound on contamination (`docs/blind-write-ledger.md` § 0). `shibajee` has no gpio, spi, wdt, led or keys driver, so several cells of the diff are absent.
- The diff found a defect in `shibajee`'s timer: four helpers read the 32-bit `TCCNR` into a `u16` while the control bits sit at 31:28, so those bits are never set, with no warning under `-Wall` (`docs/driver-diff.md` § 3.5).
