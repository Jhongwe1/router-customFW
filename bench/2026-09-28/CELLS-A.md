# CELLS-A — `R6b-8` 8c-cells, group A: the current standby, one boot of `r6b8cr`, no power action

Written 2026-09-27 (segment 115) under the owner's relaxed process for gate `R6b`: no freeze,
no prediction ceremony, test while doing. **Not a PREDICTIONS card**: it is the one source of
every command group A types, read by `tools/cardrun.py` (which runs it) and by

## § 1 The image

`r6b8cr` (= `r6b8cr2`), quiet variant, recipe `527e683b`, quiet `/init`
(`config/rlxfw-init-quiet.sh`: opens no interface, writes no `/proc` node). `nfjrom`
`/home/key/fwre-work/rebuild/s114/r6b8c/rtk/r6b8cr/rlxfw/kroot/rtkload/nfjrom`, sha256
`a6c9c818d5cb36fc04f24495c196206406ad3b84212663ae810113b45fbba7e7`, 1,171,456 bytes, its
`rtkimage` record's tripwire CLEAN; vmlinux `d57b02e2…`. Vendor tree present
(`CONFIG_RTL_819X_SWCORE=y`, `CONFIG_RTL_DEBUG_TOOL=y`, `CONFIG_RLXFW_VENDOR_ETH_OPEN=y`),
`CONFIG_PRINTK` unset (the vendor's `/proc/rtl865x` prints go through `panic_printk` and reach
the console, 量 block 51), `CONFIG_RTL_WTDOG` unset, rlxfw's watchdog with `bootguard 1`,
`hw_ovsel 9`, `kick_ms 250` (讀 `config/rlxfw-src/.../rtl819x-wdt.c`): a kernel whose timer wheel
stops is bitten about 84 s later. `looprun` pins the `nfjrom` by digest and compares the booted
`RLXFW-ID0` with `527E683B`.

## § 4 Standing rules

* **No flash write**: no `FLW`, `EW`, `EB`, non-zero `AUTOBURN`, and no `FLR`; the loader cells
  are `DW` and `MDIOR` reads only; the one upload is `looprun`'s, after it reads `00000000` back
  from the `AUTOBURN` word. Nothing touches `0x000000`–`0x007FFF`; no `H601` byte can reach a
  capture (the view refuses flash; `cardcheck`'s HW-1 refuses a memory-node footprint on flash).
* **The vendor's `/proc/rtl865x/memory`**: reads only in HW-1's one form, each footprint inside
  one window and clear of `PSRP` and `0xBB804600`; exactly the two writes the card's `memwrite`
  fence declares, both `PCRP3`, the second restoring the first.
* **No HOST cell runs `tools/viewdecode.py`**: it does not carry the `FW-124` contract, so
  `cardcheck` refuses it in a cell; its bracket and one-boot reads run at the desk after the
  captures exist (`RUN-8c.md` § 4).
* **Every board cell's `--until` carries the reset banner** and is followed by the loader-text
  gate; no Linux cell carries `--esc-after`; the loader cells and the closing `reboot` do.
  A stop anywhere after the upload opens `w8c.sh`'s tail watch at once (the drivers do it).
* **The switch page before `port_status`** (the vendor's reader clears `PSRP` bit 8 unrecorded).
* **`peek` reads only the 298 admitted words**; each run of a peek is admitted whole.
* **rlxfw's MDIO before the vendor's**, and the vendor's not at all after `reset vendor`.
* **No write to a switch register before the vendor-configured reads end** (`A-V`); the first
  is `A-N`'s `start`.
