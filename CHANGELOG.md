# Changelog

What each released version of rlxfw contains, one short section per release,
newest first. Each section summarises and links: the full section as it was
released, and the per-segment entries written until 2026-09-16, are in
[`docs/history/changelog-through-v1.0.md`](docs/history/changelog-through-v1.0.md),
moved there verbatim on 2026-10-09. Each release's assets and their sha256 are on
its GitHub release page. Versions before `v0.2` were not released
(`PROGRESS.md` § Release clock); which gates define each version is `README.md`'s
[version map](README.md#which-gates-make-which-version).

`## Unreleased` collects what the next tag will carry and is cut into a
`## vX.Y — date` section of at most 40 lines in the commit that makes the tag. A
release that carries an image names it by sha256 in its section
(`docs/release-process.md` A4).

## v1.0 — 2026-10-09

The first release with a binary: the image and its corresponding source are assets
of the same release ([`docs/offer.md`](docs/offer.md) § 0). Gates: `R6c`, `R8b`,
`R9`, `P3` and `P4b`; `R8` closed before `v0.6` was tagged and is in its section.

**Image:** `mainline-9bb2bec7.img`, sha256
`295d4f6aec14f8b6ee21ebccec05e6b5492d8785274afe676fbfa5a070fb86bd`.

- `R6c`: booted from a flash slot through `rlxboot`, rlxfw's Ethernet receives;
  `rtl819x-switch` 1.6 writes the loader's VLAN layout itself (`vlan`).
- `R8b`: an update in a flash slot boots through `rlxboot`, a power cut during a
  slot write leaves the other slot booting, and `rlxboot` carries `cs6c`.
- `R9`: [`docs/differential.md`](docs/differential.md), the differential table.
- `P3`: [`docs/bringup.md`](docs/bringup.md), the bring-up report.
- `P4b`: the licences (`NOTICE`, `LICENSE`), the per-file modification record
  generated by `tools/modrecord.py`, the source archive built by
  `tools/srcarchive.py`, and [`docs/release-process.md`](docs/release-process.md).
- `FLW-1`: `tools/digestscan.py` reads the tree for a digest of a flash window
  that overlaps `H601`.
- The weakest part: the flash path is a provisioning procedure on one unit, not an
  update the device performs.

## v0.6 — 2026-10-04

Gates: `R7`, and `R8`, closed by splitting its row after `R8a`. No binary asset.

- `R7`: rlxfw's own userspace — `init` as a compiled PID 1, `cfgstore`, `brokerd`,
  `httpd`, `dnsfwd` and `ifupd` — with busybox rebuilt to a chosen applet set;
  `tools/uspacescan.py` checks the programs' imports.
- `R8a`: `rlxboot` boots a signed update container from RAM and refuses a flipped
  payload bit and a version below the anti-rollback counter. Persistence became
  `R8b`, a row of its own.
- `R1y`: `PROGRESS.md` holds state only; its closed records moved verbatim to
  `docs/history/`, and `tools/citeresolve.py` reads a record's line citation at
  the commit that wrote it.
- `FW-184`: `brokerd` re-reads the config store before every request.
- CI, red on every head pushed on 2026-09-30, repaired step by step.
- The weakest part: all of it ran from RAM, and each device result is one boot or
  one seating of one image.

## v0.5 — 2026-09-29

Gates: `R6` and `P2`, with `R6b`. No binary asset.

- `R6`: `rtl819x-nic` and `rtl819x-switch`, an Ethernet driver of rlxfw's own;
  `rlx0` pings both ways and carries bulk TCP.
- `R6b`: the transmit fault fixed by the vendor's descriptor-length convention,
  `rtl819x-nic` 1.6's default; the vendor's Ethernet tree out of the mainline
  image.
- `P2`: `tools/boot-timeline.py` splits both firmwares' boots on one host clock
  ([`docs/boot-time-table.md`](docs/boot-time-table.md)).
- The weakest part: the switch state `D8` passed on was the loader's, measured
  only on boots that followed a TFTP upload.

## v0.4 — 2026-09-17

Gates: `R1-pub` with `R2c`, and `P1`; `R1z` closed in the same span. No binary
asset, no take.

- `R1-pub` + `R2c`: the instruction, hazard and Lexra-ASE census, the
  vendor-kernel emulation column, and the three-toolchain comparison on silicon.
- `P1`: `config/mfgtest.sh`, a production test run on this board, with checks
  made to fail by physical injection and `tools/mfginject.py`'s host-side
  injections.
- `R1z`: the debts the repository's own record names.
- Three of `P1`'s eleven design-table rows over-declare.

## v0.3 — 2026-09-11

Gates: `R4` and `R5`. No binary asset, no take.

- `R4`: edit to result as one command, with a reset that does not touch the
  power switch, and the loop's assertion derived from the build.
- `R5`: six drivers in one image — `rtl819x-timer`, `rtl819x-gpio`, `rtl819x-spi`
  with MTD, `rtl819x-wdt`, `rtl819x-keys` and upstream's `leds-gpio` — with
  compile-tested device-tree bindings marked *not probed on hardware*, and
  [`docs/driver-diff.md`](docs/driver-diff.md).

- `R3`: a kernel of rlxfw's own boots this device to a shell and pings; the
  60-second take replays committed serial captures and says so.
